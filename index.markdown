# Personal Projects

Here lies the collections of ideas I have written on.
+ [Spirographs and Groups](files/math/Spirographs.pdf) \
	Many of the proofs online involve the assumption that the spirographs are created by tracing the movement of a gear turning around another gear. Because gears have integer teeths, there is a hidden assumption that the ratio between the gear number is a rational number. The need for such an assumption had me curious. Here, this paper considers any two circles, and shows why only rational gear ratios have group properties.
+ [Stone Representation Theorem](files/math/StoneRepresentation.pdf) \
	Inspired by a book I read on Boolean Algebra, [Logic as algebra](https://doi.org/10.1090/dol/021); It has quite the long exposition on Stone's Representation Theorem. Here, I distill it into something more digestable and quicker to read for the average math audience. It concludes with different Boolean concepts being tied to a powerset.
